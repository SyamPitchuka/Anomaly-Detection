E5 – injected faults on Alibaba (second dataset, no labels)

Alibaba's cluster trace has no anomaly labels, so we inject labelled faults into real Alibaba machine metrics, replay them through the real producer and measure what a per-metric detector catches. Script: src/producer/inject_faults.py (written by Raghav Mishra, branch raghav/inject-faults); runs and write-up by Syam.

Setup
Data: Alibaba cluster trace v2018, machine_usage (public), machines m_1..m_40: 2,468,863 readings over 8 days, converted with src/producer/alibaba_to_parquet.py (raw layer, contract schema).
Slice: 40 machines, first 48 hours; the first 24 hours are fault-free and give each machine-metric its baseline (median and MAD), so the daily load cycle is part of "normal".
Faults (one per machine, 10 per type, random machine, metric and 5-minute-aligned start after the clean part):
spike: one random metric x 3 for 5 minutes
drift: one random metric +2 % per minute for 30 minutes
break: net_in x 0.1 for 15 minutes while CPU is high and untouched
Replay: the injected raw layer goes through src/producer/replay.py (event-time order) into a capture producer, so the detector sees exactly the JSON messages Kafka would carry.
Detector: Tier 1 robust z-score on 5-minute window means (score = max over metrics of |x - median| / (1.4826 MAD)), flag = score > threshold. Scored with src/detection/evaluate.py: recall = share of faults with at least one flagged window, delay = fault start to first flagged window, false alarms per machine per day on the same machines.
Seeds 7, 8, 9 (different machines, metrics and times per seed). Run on spark-master, 17 Nov 2026.

Interpretation
At the SMD default threshold of 3 the detector flags about 60 % of all windows (177 false alarms per machine per day), so its 100 % recall is meaningless. Alibaba metrics are bursty, exactly the behaviour that made raw Tier 1 unusable on SMD (report section 6.2).
Held to the same false-alarm budget as SMD (at most 2 per machine per day, threshold 20), it catches every network break, about half the spikes and 40 % of the slow drifts, and drifts only after about 14 minutes, near the end of the 30-minute ramp.
The breaks are easy here only because net_in x 0.1 pushes that single metric far outside its range. A subtler correlation break, where each metric stays within its normal range, is invisible to any per-metric score and needs a multivariate model such as the autoencoder used on SMD; training one for Alibaba is future work.
The seed spread (spike recall 0.40–0.70) shows that single-seed numbers on 10 faults per type are noisy; the threshold trade-off, not the exact recall, is the robust finding.
Commands
python src/producer/alibaba_to_parquet.py --input machine_usage_m1-m40.csv --driver-memory 3g
python src/producer/inject_faults.py --machines 40 --per-type 10 --hours 48 --clean-hours 24 --seed 7 --threshold T
    # T in 3 5 8 12 20 40 for seed 7; T in 12 20 for seeds 8 and 9

The 40-machine CSV is the rows of machine_usage.csv whose machine_id is m_1..m_40 (awk filter on the public file).
